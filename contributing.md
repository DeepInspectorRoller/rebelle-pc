## 🎨 The Ultimate Digital Painting Experience

For decades, digital artists have been confined to the rigid, perfect lines of vector graphics and the synthetic feel of digital brushes. **Rebelle Pc** is a revolutionary, physics-based painting software that completely shatters these limitations. This repository serves as the ultimate guide, toolkit, and resource hub for installing, configuring, and mastering Rebelle on your Windows PC.

Unlike traditional digital painting software, Rebelle simulates real-world fluid dynamics. Watercolors bleed and blend on the canvas just like they do on paper. Acrylics retain their texture, and pastels smudge realistically. The software uses a proprietary "NanoPixel" technology that allows you to zoom into your artwork infinitely without ever seeing pixelation.

This repository provides everything you need to get started. We offer pre-configured Wacom and Huion tablet profiles, custom paper textures, and a massive library of community-shared brush presets. Whether you are a traditional artist transitioning to digital, or a seasoned digital painter looking for a more organic feel, Rebelle Pc will completely transform your creative workflow.

## 🚀 Quick Setup

To get Rebelle running on your PC, you will need Windows 10 or 11, at least 8GB of RAM, and a graphics tablet for the best experience.

```bash
# Clone the repository to your desktop
git clone https://github.com/your-username/rebelle-pc.git

# Navigate into the directory
cd rebelle-pc

# Run the automated setup script (requires PowerShell)
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\install.ps1

# Once installed, launch Rebelle from your desktop shortcut
```

Once the software launches, you will be greeted by the intuitive workspace. Navigate to `File -> Preferences -> Tablet` to import our pre-configured tablet profiles. Then, head over to the `Paper` panel to select a custom texture from our library. You are now ready to start painting.

## 🧠 The Physics Of Digital Watercolor

What makes Rebelle so unique is its underlying physics engine. Traditional digital brushes simply stamp a pre-rendered image onto a canvas. Rebelle, however, simulates the actual behavior of liquid.

When you paint with a watercolor brush, the software calculates the amount of "water" on your brush and the absorbency of the digital paper. It simulates how the pigment spreads through the fibers of the canvas, creating the beautiful, unpredictable edges that are the hallmark of real watercolor.

The software also simulates gravity. If you rotate your canvas, the wet paint will slowly drip downwards. This level of realism is completely unprecedented in digital art. Our repository includes a detailed guide on how to calibrate the physics engine to match different real-world papers, from cold-press watercolor paper to smooth Bristol board.

## 🤝 Community & Contributing

We are a community of artists who love pushing the boundaries of digital creativity. If you have created a custom brush, designed a paper texture, or want to share your Rebelle artwork, we welcome your contributions!

1. **Fork** the repository.
2. **Create** your feature branch (`git checkout -b feature/new-brush`).
3. **Commit** your changes (`git commit -m 'Add new brush preset'`).
4. **Push** to the branch (`git push origin feature/new-brush`).
5. **Open** a Pull Request.

Please read our `CONTRIBUTING.md` for detailed guidelines on submitting brushes and paper textures. We also have an active Discord server where you can share your artwork, ask for help with tablet settings, and discuss the latest updates in the software.
